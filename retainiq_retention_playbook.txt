COMPANY OVERVIEW

RetainIQ is an AI-powered customer retention platform that combines predictive churn scoring (ChurnSense) with a RAG-powered intelligence layer (this system) to give retention teams real-time, explainable, actionable insights. The platform serves telecom companies, subscription businesses, and any organisation facing customer churn risk.

RetainIQ's two core products:
1. ChurnSense — ML model that scores every customer's churn probability in real time with SHAP explainability
2. RetainIQ Assistant — RAG-powered chatbot that answers strategic and operational questions using ChurnSense's own analysis

RETENTION PLAYBOOK — RISK-BASED INTERVENTION FRAMEWORK

TIER 1 — CRITICAL RISK (Churn probability 70%+)
Trigger: ChurnSense score ≥ 0.70
Profile: Month-to-Month contract + tenure under 6 months + 3+ support calls
Action: Immediate personal outreach within 48 hours
Channel: Direct phone call from senior retention agent
Offer: Annual contract upgrade with 2-month discount (approximately €70 value)
Script focus: Acknowledge support call history, offer dedicated account manager, present annual plan savings
Target: 30–35% retention rate on this segment
Expected monthly volume: approximately 800–1,200 customers

TIER 2 — HIGH RISK (Churn probability 40–70%)
Trigger: ChurnSense score 0.40–0.70
Profile: Month-to-Month contract OR short tenure (6–12 months) with 1–2 support calls
Action: Targeted digital outreach within 5 business days
Channel: Email + in-app notification
Offer: Loyalty reward (e.g., one month at 50% price) or feature upgrade
Script focus: Highlight unused features, reinforce value proposition, introduce annual plan
Target: 20–25% retention rate on this segment
Expected monthly volume: approximately 2,000–3,000 customers

TIER 3 — MODERATE RISK (Churn probability 20–40%)
Trigger: ChurnSense score 0.20–0.40
Profile: Annual contract approaching renewal OR moderate support call history
Action: Proactive engagement campaign
Channel: Email newsletter, SMS, push notification
Offer: Early renewal discount, referral programme invitation
Script focus: Product education, satisfaction survey, upcoming feature announcements
Target: 40–50% retention rate (lower intervention intensity, higher baseline retention)
Expected monthly volume: approximately 4,000–6,000 customers

TIER 4 — LOW RISK (Churn probability below 20%)
Trigger: ChurnSense score < 0.20
Profile: Long-tenure annual contract customers, high engagement
Action: Standard engagement and upsell
Channel: Regular newsletter, loyalty programme
Offer: Upsell to premium tier, referral bonus
Target: Standard retention — these customers are largely self-sustaining

HIGH-PRIORITY RETENTION ACTIONS (RANKED BY ROI)

ACTION 1: Convert Month-to-Month customers to Annual contracts
- Impact: Reduces churn risk by up to 19x (from 45.2% to 2.4%)
- Target: All Month-to-Month customers, prioritise those with tenure under 12 months
- Offer: 1–2 months free on annual plan, or 15–20% annual discount
- Expected conversion rate: 15–25% of targeted customers
- This single action is the highest-ROI retention initiative available

ACTION 2: Intensive onboarding programme for new customers (first 90 days)
- Impact: Addresses the 41% churn rate in first 6 months
- Activities: Welcome call at day 3, feature walkthrough at day 14, check-in call at day 45, satisfaction survey at day 60
- Target: ALL new customers, with extra touchpoints for Month-to-Month contracts
- Expected outcome: Reduce early churn rate from 41% to approximately 28–32%

ACTION 3: Support call escalation protocol
- Trigger: Customer reaches 3rd support call in 6-month window
- Action: Automatic escalation to senior retention agent; next contact is proactive (agent calls customer, not vice versa)
- Script: Acknowledge frustration, offer dedicated support contact, present retention offer
- Expected outcome: 35–40% reduction in churn among the 3+ calls segment

ACTION 4: Proactive contract renewal campaign (60 days before expiry)
- Target: Annual contract customers approaching renewal
- Channel: Email + phone
- Offer: Early renewal discount (10–15%) plus loyalty recognition
- Expected outcome: 85–90% renewal rate on targeted segment (vs ~70% without intervention)

CUSTOMER RISK PROFILES — QUICK REFERENCE

PROFILE A: "The Early Leaver" — HIGHEST RISK
- Tenure: Under 6 months
- Contract: Month-to-Month
- Support calls: 2+
- ChurnSense score: typically 75–90%
- Recommended action: Tier 1 intervention, personal call within 48 hours
- Key message: "We noticed you've had some questions — let us make this right"

PROFILE B: "The Frustrated Long-Timer"
- Tenure: 12–36 months
- Contract: Month-to-Month (never upgraded)
- Support calls: 3+
- ChurnSense score: typically 55–75%
- Recommended action: Tier 2 intervention, loyalty recognition + annual plan offer
- Key message: "You've been with us for [X] months — here's what we'd like to offer you as a valued customer"

PROFILE C: "The Dissatisfied New Annual"
- Tenure: Under 12 months
- Contract: Annual
- Satisfaction score: Low (under 5/10)
- Support calls: 2+
- ChurnSense score: typically 40–60%
- Recommended action: Tier 2 intervention, satisfaction recovery call
- Key message: "We want to make sure you're getting full value from your plan"

PROFILE D: "The Stable Long-Timer" — LOWEST RISK
- Tenure: 3+ years
- Contract: Annual or Two-Year
- Support calls: 0–1
- ChurnSense score: typically under 15%
- Recommended action: Tier 4, upsell opportunity
- Key message: Loyalty recognition, referral programme

ETHICAL GUIDELINES FOR RETENTION AI

The following guidelines govern the use of ChurnSense scores in retention operations:

1. HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED: No adverse decision (e.g., withholding service, denying upgrade) may be made based solely on ChurnSense score without human agent review. Compliant with GDPR Article 22 (right not to be subject to automated decision-making).

2. TRANSPARENCY: Customers have the right to know that AI-assisted scoring is used in retention processes. RetainIQ recommends disclosing this in privacy policies.

3. BIAS MONITORING: Gender and region features have been confirmed as near-zero SHAP contributors and are flagged for removal in ChurnSense v2. Regular bias audits should be conducted quarterly.

4. EU AI ACT CLASSIFICATION: ChurnSense is classified as a limited-risk AI system under the EU AI Act. Transparency obligations apply — customers should be informed when interacting with AI-assisted processes.

5. DATA MINIMISATION: Only features with confirmed predictive value should be used in production scoring. Gender and region will be removed in v2 to reduce data collection scope and address fairness concerns.

6. EXPLAINABILITY: Every ChurnSense prediction comes with SHAP-based explanations accessible to retention agents. No "black box" decisions — every score can be explained in plain English.

TECHNOLOGY STACK

ChurnSense (Task 1 — AI Product):
- Model: Logistic Regression with StandardScaler pipeline
- Training data: 50,000 telecom customers, 22 features
- API: FastAPI /predict endpoint, returns probability + SHAP values
- Frontend: Streamlit interface for retention agents

RetainIQ Assistant (Task 2 — RAG System):
- Document ingestion: LangChain RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter (500 chars, 100 overlap)
- Embeddings: sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384-dimensional vectors)
- Vector database: FAISS (local, CPU-based)
- LLM backend: Claude 3 Haiku via OpenRouter
- Frontend: Streamlit chat interface with source citations
- Out-of-scope handling: L2 distance threshold prevents hallucination on unknown queries
